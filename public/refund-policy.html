Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

1Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for on-chain payments made through Arcodian and Arc Pay (the "Service"). It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. An original blockchain settlement cannot be reversed; an approved refund is a new merchant-funded transaction.

2Eligibility for a refund

You may request a refund if:

  • You were charged an incorrect amount, or charged more than once for the same order;
  • The paid product or service was not delivered or was materially not as described; or
  • A technical error on our side caused a payment to fail to deliver its intended result.

Refunds are generally not available for completed and correctly delivered orders, for changes of mind after delivery of a digital item, or where required by an applicable promotion's stated terms.

3How to request a refund

Send your request to support@arcodian.fun within 7 days of the transaction, including: the transaction reference or hash, the date and amount, the payment method used, and a short description of the issue. We may ask for additional information to verify the request.

4Processing & timing

  • On-chain payments: blockchain transactions are irreversible. Where a refund is approved, the merchant submits a new transaction returning the gross invoice amount to the payer address recorded by Arc Pay.
  • Fees: the original protocol fee and network costs are not reversed. The merchant funds the gross refund and its new transaction cost.

We aim to review every refund request within 3 business days and will notify you of the outcome.

5Cancellations

An order may be cancelled before it is settled or delivered. Once a payment is confirmed and the product or service is delivered, the Refund Policy above applies instead.

6Contact

Questions about refunds? Contact support@arcodian.fun or reach us through our Discord community.
